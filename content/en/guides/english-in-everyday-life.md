# Give English a place in everyday life

Build a sustainable English habit around interests and everyday tasks.

## Start with an interest

Watch a sports interview, read a recipe or use documentation for work. Let English help with something you already want to do.

## Make a small version

Aim to understand one answer or explain one event. Use this smaller task on busy days and leave a clear starting point for next time.

## Add your own response

Respond to one relevant question: do you agree, have a similar experience or see another approach? A few sentences are enough.

I watched an interview about handling mistakes.
It reminded me of a rushed release. Next time, I will check the migration first.

## Move from self-talk to exchange

Use self-talk to prepare, then invite a detail question from an AI or friend. Practise responding and clarifying as well as delivering a monologue.

## Place feedback thoughtfully

For conversation, finish the thought before correcting one issue. For accuracy, pause and retry while preserving your meaning and tone.

## Restart with a small task

Do not repay missed sessions. Shorten the task or choose easier material, then build up again.
