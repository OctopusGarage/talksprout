# Four strands of language learning

Combine understanding, expression, language study and fluency.

## Input: understand a message

Choose material you can broadly follow and want to continue. Follow the message, checking only what blocks understanding.

## Output: express your meaning

Speak or write before seeing a model. Define the audience and purpose to make the task useful.

## Study: investigate a difficulty

Choose a word, collocation or structure from an actual difficulty. Check its use and make an example of your own.

## Fluency: rely less on a script

Use familiar material for smoother listening, retelling or writing. Move from keywords to no script, then try a related situation. A retelling or timed explanation can be a focused task; timing is optional.

## Example: explain a delay

Read a project update, explain a delay yourself, check one useful expression and repeat without a script. One task can connect all four strands.

The integration is taking longer than expected.
We ran into an authentication issue.
I expect to have an update tomorrow.

## Plan across the week

You do not need equal parts in every session. Look across the week for input, output, focused study and familiar practice, and add what is missing.
