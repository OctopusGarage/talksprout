# Reading and comprehension checks

Read first, ask about a difficulty and check your understanding.

## Choose a reading tool

Use Reading guide for a passage, Unpack a complex sentence for structure, and Reading comprehension check for self-testing.

## State the main idea

Summarise a short passage in one sentence and mark what blocks understanding. Look for actions and dates in emails, and prerequisites and limits in technical documents.

## Ask a focused question

Share the sentence and your interpretation. Ask about the specific difference; for long sentences, find the main clause before modifiers and references.

## Answer without the explanation

Read before answering and ask for a hint when stuck. If you have already seen the answers, try another passage to check independent understanding.
