# A short AI speaking practice loop

Practise one situation and keep a few useful expressions.

## Choose a situation

Start with a work conversation, travel experience or everyday topic. Chat freely or choose a scenario in Role-play Coach.

## Set the goal

Tell the AI what you want to achieve, such as explaining a delay or resolving a booking problem. Treat ten minutes as a flexible guide.

I need to explain a project delay. Help me make the next step clear.

## Choose the feedback rhythm

For flow, speak before receiving feedback. For accuracy, pause to fix one issue. Ask for a hint and continue in your own words.

## Finish with a small review

Choose one to three useful expressions and use them in another situation. Revisit one at your next session.
