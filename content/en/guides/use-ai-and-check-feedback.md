# Use AI tools and check their feedback

Choose instructions, supply the material and check the feedback.

## Send the practice instructions

Use the full prompt when pasting is convenient, or the short spoken opening otherwise. With a Skill or MCP connection, choose the task directly in your AI.

## Ask for help as needed

Ask for a hint, another topic, a recap or an end to practice. Choose a review tool when you want full notes or cards.

## Check the source and feedback

Supply material directly to your AI. Check that revisions preserve meaning, time and tone, and ask for the basis of a correction. Text alone cannot establish actual pronunciation.
