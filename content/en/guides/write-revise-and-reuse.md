# Write, revise and reuse

Write your version, understand revisions and reuse the language.

## Start or revise

Choose Scenario writing practice for a new piece or Writing feedback for a draft. Dedicated helpers cover email, workplace messages and summaries.

## Define the reader and purpose

Decide what the reader should know or do, then write a short draft. Keep facts, estimates and requests distinct.

## Understand and revise

Focus on one to three important issues. Check time, tone and meaning, revise yourself and ask for another check.

## Try another situation

Hide the model and write for another reader or situation. Check summaries against the original rather than adding unsupported ideas.
