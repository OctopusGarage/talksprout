# English learning evidence and tool guide checks

Reviewed on 15 November 2026. These findings support editorial decisions for a proposed TalkSprout knowledge section. Verification consisted of reading research and official documentation; no AI provider, audio workflow, or browser behavior was tested.

## Learning principles

**Balance learning activities.** Paul Nation distinguishes meaning-focused input, meaning-focused output, deliberate language study, and fluency development with familiar material. He proposes roughly equal time across these strands for a balanced course. Use this as a planning framework rather than a personalized optimum or a requirement that every short session contain all four. His guide also recommends spaced repetition, retrieval and varied encounters. [Nation, What do you need to know to learn a foreign language?, pp. 8–9 and 51–52](https://www.wgtn.ac.nz/lals/resources/paul-nations-resources/paul-nations-publications/publications/documents/foreign-language_1125.pdf).

**Space practice and recall before checking.** A 2022 review reports benefits of spacing and retrieval practice across learning domains and ages. This supports revisiting expressions over time and attempting an answer before revealing it. It does not establish a universal English learning schedule, a required repetition count, or guaranteed transfer from vocabulary recall to spontaneous conversation. [Carpenter, Pan and Butler, Nature Reviews Psychology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s44159-022-00089-1).

**Remove learning pyramid retention percentages.** Lalley and Miller's investigation found no credible research supporting the pyramid's retention hierarchy. Their examination of Dale's books found no empirical retention percentages. Do not publish claims that learners retain fixed percentages from reading, listening, speaking or teaching. This objection does not imply that practice or explanation is useless. [Lalley and Miller, The learning pyramid: Does it point teachers in the right direction?](https://go.gale.com/ps/i.do?asid=0582e110&id=GALE%7CA169960867&it=r&p=AONE&u=googlescholar&v=2.1).

**Avoid fixed visual or auditory learner labels.** The EEF evidence synthesis reports insufficient rigorous evidence for matching instruction to assigned learning styles and warns against restricting learners to those categories. Its scope is school education; it is not a direct adult English learning trial. Allow material preferences while choosing activities according to the skill and task. [EEF Learning styles](https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/learning-styles).

## Gemini guide corrections

**A custom Gem is not a Gemini Live configuration.** Google's current help says Gems cannot be used with Gemini Live, and the premade Learning coach Gem does not currently support language learning. The Live product FAQ separately excludes access to Gems and Notebooks. A guide must not present creating an English Coach Gem and opening Live as one verified workflow. [Use Gems in Gemini Apps](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/15146780?hl=en), [Gemini Live FAQ](https://gemini.google/us/overview/gemini-live/?hl=en).

**Version tool instructions by review date.** Google says personal-account Gems start transitioning to skills in November 2026, with work and school accounts following in 2027. Skills provide reusable custom instructions; current help lists an age requirement of 18 or over. This is a documented transition, not proof that every account has the same interface or that skills operate in Live. Prefer a provider-independent learning workflow with a dated, account-specific setup appendix. [Transition from Gems to skills](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/18560919?hl=en), [Create and manage skills](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17094296?hl=en).

## External resource scope

**The old English guide URL now points to a broader book.** Opening `byoungd/English-level-up-tips` redirects to `byoungd/up`. The current README describes a bilingual lifelong learning guide covering English, AI, projects, entrepreneurship and life. Its English section remains relevant, but importing or recommending the whole repository as an English-only methodology collection would misrepresent its scope. The README labels book content CC BY-NC 4.0, so retain attribution and check the relevant content license before republication. Prefer links to selected English chapters and an original, attributed summary. [Current repository](https://github.com/byoungd/up).

## Editorial application

Publish practical actions with a clear purpose, preparation, steps and a way to check the result. Label personal routines and video summaries as experience-based suggestions. Treat fixed time budgets, exact repetition counts, universal vocabulary thresholds and unsupported brain-science explanations as claims requiring their own evidence. Keep source verification separate from real model execution and audio compatibility testing.
