## Choose the workflow
Resolve requested coach, review or listening/reading/writing task names through prompts/index.md first. Use guides/index.md when the learner asks for learning-method explanations or reusable configurations; articles are not substitutes for practice tasks, even when titles are similar. Offer actual index choices when the request is broad. For conversations select a coach and optional compatible scenario; mock interview documents explicitly defer feedback. Reviews use the current accessible chat or the speaker-labeled transcript template. Listening, reading and writing documents specify required source material. Guides explain methods and provide reusable configurations. Follow explicit help, review and stop requests.
